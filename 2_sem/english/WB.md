# Wildberries Presentation Script

---

## Slide 0 – Title Slide

Hello everyone.  
Today I will present a company called Wildberries.

---

## Slide 1 – General Information

Wildberries was founded in 2004 by Tatyana Bakalchuk in Moscow, Russia.  
The company is headquartered in Moscow and operates across Russia, CIS countries, and Europe.  
Its slogan is “Wildberries — everything you need.”  
The company employs over 400,000 people and is one of the largest private employers in Russia.

---

## Slide 2 – Product / Service Range

Wildberries is a large online marketplace with over 1,000 product categories.  
It sells fashion items, electronics, home goods, beauty products, and groceries.  
The platform originally focused on clothing but has expanded into many areas.  
It also offers delivery and pickup services through thousands of locations.

---

## Slide 3 – Financial Performance

Wildberries shows strong revenue growth and reached about 8.5 billion dollars in GMV in 2023.  
The company usually operates with low margins because it reinvests in logistics and expansion.  
Its EBITDA has been positive in recent years.  
Overall, the company has strong cash flow due to high sales volume.

---

## Slide 4 – Market Share

Wildberries is the leader in Russia’s e-commerce market with around 35% market share.  
Its main competitor is Ozon, which is rapidly expanding.  
Another growing competitor is Yandex Market.  
The company is also expanding internationally, especially in Eastern Europe and Central Asia.

---

## Slide 5 – Advertising and Promotion

Wildberries focuses on digital marketing instead of traditional advertising.  
It активно uses social media, influencers, and online platforms.  
The company has strong brand recognition, with over 90% awareness among Russian users.  
It also uses loyalty programs and thousands of pickup points to strengthen its brand.

---

## Slide 6 – Reasons for Success

Wildberries’ success is based on strong logistics and a large network of pickup points.  
Its marketplace model reduces risks and allows many sellers to join the platform.  
The company also benefited from early entry into the market.  
However, it faces competition and needs to continue innovating.  
Overall, Wildberries has strong growth potential in the future.
